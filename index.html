Our Story

A Century of
Academic Excellence

Founded in 1887 by Sir Edmund Ashford, our university has grown from a modest college of 40 students into one of the world's most respected academic institutions. Spanning 300 acres of historic grounds, we champion a culture of curiosity, inclusivity, and relentless intellectual pursuit.

From Nobel laureates to world leaders, Ashford alumni have left an indelible mark on science, culture, policy, and business — a legacy we continue to build upon every day.

About Ashford

Our History

Over 135 Years of
Academic Tradition

1887

Founded by Sir Edmund Ashford

Established as Ashford College with 40 students and four faculty members in Oxford, England.

1920

University Charter Granted

Royal Charter elevated the institution to full university status, enabling degree-conferring powers.

1965

Faculty of Sciences Established

A landmark £3M investment created state-of-the-art science facilities, attracting global researchers.

1998

International Expansion

Partnership programmes with 60 universities worldwide solidified Ashford's global identity.

2024

Innovation Hub Opens

A £180M state-of-the-art innovation centre for AI, biotech, and sustainable technology research.

🎯

Our Mission

Our Vision

Admissions

Entry Requirements

Undergraduate

  • ✓ 3 A-Levels (ABB minimum, AAA for competitive programmes)
  • ✓ GCSE English Language and Mathematics at Grade 5+
  • ✓ International Baccalaureate: 34–38 points
  • ✓ English Proficiency: IELTS 6.5+ (no band below 6.0)
  • ✓ Personal Statement and Academic Reference

Postgraduate

  • ✓ Relevant Bachelor's degree (2:1 or above)
  • ✓ IELTS 7.0+ for taught programmes
  • ✓ Research proposal (for PhD applicants)
  • ✓ Two academic references
  • ✓ CV / Professional Portfolio (for MBA)

Application Process

01

Choose Your Programme

Browse our 320+ programmes and identify the degree that aligns with your ambitions and qualifications.

02

Complete the Application

Submit via UCAS (UK undergraduates) or our Direct Application Portal. Include all required documents.

03

Await Our Decision

Our admissions team reviews applications within 4–6 weeks. You'll receive a conditional or unconditional offer.

04

Meet Your Conditions

Submit final grades and proof of qualifications to convert your conditional offer.

05

Enrol & Begin

Complete online enrolment, arrange accommodation, and join the Ashford community!
