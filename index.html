Our Story

A Century of
Academic Excellent

Founded in 1887 by Sir James Ashford, our university has grown from a modest college of 40 students into one of the world's most respected academic institutions. Spanning 300 acres of historic grounds, we champion a culture of curiosity, inclusivity, and relentless intellectual pursuit.

From Nobel laureates to world leaders, Ashford alumni have left an indelible mark on science, culture, policy, and business — a legacy we continue to build upon every day.
